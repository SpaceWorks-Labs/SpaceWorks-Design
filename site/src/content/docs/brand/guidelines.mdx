The brand guidelines are the source of truth for the logo, colour, type, icons and voice. Everything else
on this site follows them.

There are two books. **Version 2** is part of Space Works Design System v2.0: it keeps everything from v1 that is
still true and adds light and dark, motion and the product. Where the two differ, v2 wins. **Version 1** is kept
unchanged for reference.
