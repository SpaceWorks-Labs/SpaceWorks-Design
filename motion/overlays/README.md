# Overlays: dialog, drawer, sheet, menu, scrim

One set of enter and exit animations for every layer that appears over the page: the scrim (fade), the dialog (pop), the phone bottom sheet (rise), side drawers (slide from their edge) and anchored menus and popovers (drop 4 px). Layers carry `data-state="open|closed"`; a presence hook keeps a layer mounted for its exit and makes it inert while it closes.

Open [`demo.html`](demo.html) in a browser (it links `../../tokens/tokens.css`) and use "Toggle dark" to check both themes.

## Files

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `overlays.css` | The `sw-fx-fade`, `sw-fx-pop`, `sw-fx-drawer`, `sw-fx-menu` rules and every keyframe, verbatim from the app. |
| `demo.html` | A standalone page with the live animation in light and dark, replayable. |
| `react/` | Copies of the real app files (see `react/README.md` for the imports to replace). |
| `previews/` | Animated WebP and GIF previews, light and dark (regenerated by `scripts/make-previews.mjs`). |

## Where the app uses it

- Fade (scrim): behind every dialog and drawer (`components/ui/Modal.tsx`, `DetailDrawer.tsx`, the docs drawer scrim in `features/docs/docs.css`).
- Pop (dialog): `components/ui/Modal.tsx`, `components/layouts/FilterPopover.tsx`, `features/presence/PresenceCheckInSheet.tsx`. On phones (under 640 px) the same class becomes the bottom sheet (`sw-sheet-in/out`).
- Drawer: `components/ui/DetailDrawer.tsx` (right edge), `components/shell/SidebarDrawer.tsx` and `features/docs/DocsLayout.tsx` (left edge, `data-edge="left"`).
- Menu: `components/ui/OverflowMenu.tsx`, the user menu, popovers; `data-above` flips the 4 px offset when the menu opens upward.
- Hook: `lib/motion/usePresence.ts` keeps the layer mounted until its exit finishes. Tooltips do not animate (they are native or instant).

## Why

A layer that appears from just in front (dialog), from the edge it docks to (drawer, sheet) or from its trigger (menu) tells the person where it came from and where it goes when closed. Exits are shorter than entrances so closing never makes anyone wait. Focus lands on something that visibly appeared.

## Timing, easing and distance

| Layer | In | Out | Distance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fade (scrim) | `--sw-dur-base` 180 ms, standard | `--sw-dur-fast` 120 ms, exit | none |
| Pop (dialog) | 180 ms, enter | 120 ms, exit | scale `--sw-pop-scale` 0.98 |
| Sheet (phones) | 180 ms, enter | 120 ms, exit | translateY 2 x `--sw-enter-y` (16 px) |
| Drawer | `--sw-dur-slow` 240 ms, enter | 180 ms, exit | translateX `--sw-slide-x` (100 %), mirrored for `data-edge="left"` |
| Menu | 120 ms, enter | 120 ms, exit | translateY `--sw-pop-offset` 4 px |

## Trigger

Set `data-state="open"` when the layer shows. To close, set `data-state="closed"`, make the layer inert, and remove it on `animationend` (or after the exit time plus 80 ms). Without animation support the layer unmounts at once.

## Reduced motion

All of it sits inside `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)`; the app also zeroes every duration under reduced motion (`styles/motion.css`) and the JS hooks skip the exit wait, so layers unmount at once. The layer appears and disappears instantly; the information (it opened, it closed) is carried by focus and content.

## Dark theme

Colour-neutral. The scrim and panel colours come from tokens (`--color-scrim`, `--color-panel`), so dark uses the black family and `--color-line` borders instead of shadows.

## Do and don't

- Do give every layer a closed state and an exit.
- Do make a closing layer inert so it cannot take focus.
- Do keep exits shorter than entrances.
- Do not unmount a layer instantly when animations are available.
- Do not slide or scale on kiosk and TV screens (the tokens zero the distances; only the fade remains).
- Do not animate layout properties.

## Copy-paste usage

```html
<link rel="stylesheet" href="../../tokens/tokens.css"><link rel="stylesheet" href="overlays.css">
<div class="sw-fx-fade" data-state="open"> <!-- scrim -->
  <div class="sw-fx-pop" data-state="open" role="dialog" aria-modal="true">…</div>
</div>
<aside class="sw-fx-drawer" data-edge="left" data-state="open">…</aside>
<ul class="sw-fx-menu" data-state="open" role="menu">…</ul>
```

Close by setting `data-state="closed"` and `inert`, then remove the node on `animationend` (see `demo.html`).
