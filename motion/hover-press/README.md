# Hover and press

The rule for pointer feedback on product chrome: hover and active change colour (120 ms) and nothing else; pressing a button sinks it to 0.98. Cards, rows, tabs and links follow the colour-only rule. The Space Works mark's box-open is the one deliberate exception (see box-open).

Open [`demo.html`](demo.html) in a browser (it links `../../tokens/tokens.css`) and use "Toggle dark" to check both themes.

## Files

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `hover-press.css` | The button transition and press rule verbatim from `styles/motion.css`, plus the colour-only hover classes the app writes per component (`.sw-hover`, `.sw-card-hover`, `.sw-row-hover`, `.sw-link-hover`) and a small example skin. |
| `demo.html` | A standalone page with the live animation in light and dark, replayable. |
| `react/` | Copies of the real app files (see `react/README.md` for the imports to replace). |
| `previews/` | Animated WebP and GIF previews, light and dark (regenerated by `scripts/make-previews.mjs`). |

## Where the app uses it

- Buttons and tabs: every `desk-button*`, `desk-tab` and `desk-nav-item` (`styles/motion.css`), sidebar items (`components/shell/SidebarItem.tsx` with `motion-safe:active:scale`).
- Cards, tabs, rows and links: `features/landing/SpaceCard.tsx`, `features/hub/hub.css`, `features/hub/featuresPage/features.css`, `features/docs/docs.css` (nav links, pager, TOC), `features/discover/spaces/spaces.css`, `components/layouts/SettingsNav.tsx`, `features/staff/SettingsSectionNav.tsx`.
- Hover opacity: `features/staff/eventProgramme/AnalyticsColumns.tsx` (bars to 80 %).

## Why

The control answers the pointer without moving the page: colour tells you what is under the cursor, the 0.98 sink tells you the press registered. Because nothing translates, scales or lifts on hover, dense staff screens never jitter and layouts never shift.

## Timing, easing and distance

| Part | Value |
|---|---|
| Colour, background, border | `--sw-dur-fast` 120 ms, `--sw-ease-standard` |
| Press | `transform: scale(0.98)` on `:active` (not disabled), same 120 ms |
| Focus ring | untouched, never animated |

## Trigger

Hover, `:active`. Press only runs under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`.

## Reduced motion

Colour still changes (the duration is 0): the state is shown, only the easing is removed. The press sink is not applied.

## Dark theme

Hover fills are the dark-family tokens (`--color-hover`, `--color-accent-hover`); the rule is the same.

## Do and don't

- Do change colour only on hover.
- Do keep focus rings out of the transition.
- Do not translate, scale, lift or add a shadow on hover.
- Do not animate on touch beyond the colour of the pressed state.
