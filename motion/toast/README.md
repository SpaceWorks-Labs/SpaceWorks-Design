# Toast

A status toast arrives from just below its slot (12 px, fading in) and leaves by dropping 8 px while fading. It never steals focus; the message is announced through a polite live region (errors through an alert region).

Open [`demo.html`](demo.html) in a browser (it links `../../tokens/tokens.css`) and use "Toggle dark" to check both themes.

## Files

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `toast.css` | The `sw-fx-toast` rule and its two keyframes, copied unchanged from `styles/motionOverlay.css`. |
| `demo.html` | A standalone page with the live animation in light and dark, replayable. |
| `react/` | Copies of the real app files (see `react/README.md` for the imports to replace). |
| `previews/` | Animated WebP and GIF previews, light and dark (regenerated by `scripts/make-previews.mjs`). |

## Where the app uses it

- `components/ui/ToastHost.tsx` (the one host mounted by the app shell): every save, undo, error and result message in staff, member and public flows.
- `lib/motion/usePresence.ts` (`useExitingItems`) keeps a dismissed toast on screen, inert, while its exit plays.

## Why

Status appears without taking focus or moving anything else, and its short exit confirms the dismissal. The tone (error colour) is carried by colour and text, never by shaking or bouncing.

## Timing, easing and distance

| Phase | Value |
|---|---|
| In | `--sw-dur-base` 180 ms, `--sw-ease-enter`, translateY 1.5 x `--sw-enter-y` (12 px) and opacity |
| Out | `--sw-dur-fast` 120 ms, `--sw-ease-exit`, translateY `--sw-enter-y` (8 px) and opacity |

## Trigger

A toast is added to the store (`data-state="open"`) or dismissed (`data-state="closed"` plus `inert`, removed on animation end).

## Reduced motion

Inside `no-preference`: the toast appears and disappears instantly; the live region still announces it.

## Dark theme

Colour-neutral; the panel, border and error text colours are tokens.

## Do and don't

- Do announce through `role="status"` (polite) or `role="alert"` for errors.
- Do keep the toast out of the focus order while it leaves (`inert`).
- Do not shake, bounce or auto-focus a toast.
- Do not add sound.

## Copy-paste usage

```html
<link rel="stylesheet" href="../../tokens/tokens.css"><link rel="stylesheet" href="toast.css">
<div class="sw-fx-toast" data-state="open" role="status">Saved.</div>
```

To dismiss: set `data-state="closed"` and `inert`, then remove the element on `animationend`.
