# Feedback: stamp, result, confetti

> **Current rule (v2.0.1, 2026-11-13):** Feedback is caused by a completed action. The demo initial Verified label is still, active replays are ignored, confetti cannot overlap, and status is announced without moving focus. Bursts remove themselves after actual animation completion. All durations/easings are token-derived; decorative success moments do not block input. Supersedes the retained implementation/timing claims below; see [docs/current-rules.md#cr-motion-01](../../docs/current-rules.md#cr-motion-01) and [motion-flow](../../docs/motion-flow.md#feedback).

How the product confirms something worked. A valid card check lands with one stamp; a result panel arrives with a small scale-in; a pass tears in from above; the workshop logbook opens like a cover; a completed return fires one confetti burst (only with interface effects on). Errors do not bounce or shake: they appear.

Open [`demo.html`](demo.html) in a browser (it links `../../tokens/tokens.css`) and use "Toggle dark" to check both themes.

## Where the app uses it

- Stamp: `features/cards/CardVerifyPage.tsx`, `cardVerify.css` (valid card check).
- Confetti: `components/delight/ReturnConfetti.tsx` inside `SuccessCard.tsx` after a return is confirmed.
- Result, pass, logbook: `components/delight/delight.css` on `ResultPanel` / `PassState.tsx` / `WorkshopLogbook.tsx`.
- The primary success feedback everywhere else is a status toast (see toast).

## Why

A confirmed result should feel confirmed, once, without delaying anyone. The stamp echoes a physical handover; the confetti is a rare celebration of a completed return. None of it carries information the text does not already carry.

## Timing, easing and distance

> **Current rule (v2.0.1, 2026-11-13):** The retained table/snippet below records the original app copy. Use the current executable files and the [complete flow recipe](../../docs/motion-flow.md#feedback) for decision 399; see [current rules](../../docs/current-rules.md#cr-motion-01).

| Animation | Duration | Easing |
|---|---|---|
| `sw-verify-stamp` | 420 ms, once | `cubic-bezier(0.32,0.72,0,1)`; scale 1.35 to 0.96 to 1, rotate -8 deg to 1 deg to 0 |
| `sw-confetti-burst` | 1100 ms, per piece delay 0, 30, 60, 90 ms | `cubic-bezier(0.2,0.7,0.3,1)`; 16 pieces in a fixed fan |
| `sw-result-arrive` | 360 ms | `ease-out`; scale .96 to 1 |
| `sw-pass-tear` | 360 ms | `ease-out`; translateY -8 px to 0 |
| `sw-logbook-open` | 360 ms | `ease-out`; rotateY -8 deg to 0 (perspective 600 px) |

## Trigger

Mount of the result. Confetti is gated: interface effects on, motion allowed, and never on kiosk, scanner, checkout or payment paths. The stamp is also gated by effects on.

## Reduced motion

Everything is off: the stamp is inside `no-preference`, the delight classes set `animation: none`, confetti is `display: none`. The text and colour of the result carry the meaning.

## Dark theme

Confetti colours are brand tokens (`--color-accent`, `--color-emphasis`, `--color-success`, `--color-warn`, `--color-info`); on a citron or yellow panel two of them swap for the panel's ink so pieces never vanish. The stamp follows the result colours.

## Do and don't

- Do keep it once and under half a second (the burst is the one longer piece).
- Do make it decorative and `aria-hidden`.
- Do not use confetti in staff, kiosk, scanner, checkout or payment flows.
- Do not animate validation errors (they appear, they do not bounce).
- Do not add sound.
