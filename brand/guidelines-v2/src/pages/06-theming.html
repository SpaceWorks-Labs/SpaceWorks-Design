Theming rules.

What changes between themes is values, artwork and edges. Names, layout and motion stay the same.

01 · One class

Dark is html.dark. Every colour is a token redefined under :root.dark. Components read tokens, never hex.

02 · No flash

A pre-paint script reads the saved choice before first paint. theme-color is {{hex:light.bg}} light and {{hex:dark.bg}} dark.

03 · Artwork per theme

Logo: full colour on light, reversed Cream on dark. Patch: mascot/dark/. Box: the .dk drawing. Illustrations ship as twins.

04 · Inks, not fills

Coloured text and icons use *-ink. Teal as text on black is {{hex:dark.accent-ink}}, never {{hex:dark.accent}}.

05 · Edges on black

Cards and the sidebar get a 1 px line. Anything a person must find (inputs, segmented controls) uses outline, at least 3 : 1.

06 · Layers, not glow

Raise by one lightness step: 0, 11, 20, 28. Never skip a step to make something pop, and never add a custom “elevated” grey.

07 · Islands rejoin

A Deep Ink panel inside a light page (.sw-island-dark) becomes the black family when the page goes dark. No green fields on black.

08 · Scrims and shadows

Scrim is Deep Ink in light, black in dark. A dark dialog separates by being a raised panel with a line, not by its shadow.
