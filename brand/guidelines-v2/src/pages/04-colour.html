Why
  1. 01Two families on one screen fight. Black beside dark green reads as a mistake.
  2. 02Tinted surfaces cost contrast. Teal on a teal-lit panel is dim; on neutral black it reads.
  3. 03Brand colour works as an accent, not a floor. Teal, Citron and Orange now stand out.
  4. 04True black is calmer and cheaper on OLED phones.

Token names never changed, so no screen needed edits. Ink, focus, accent and status fills are unchanged.

Fills are not inks.

A fill is a background with its own label. An ink is a colour you read. Never use one for the other.
