Lucide, pinned. The product draws icons with lucide, which already meets the v1 spec: 24 grid, 2 px stroke, round caps and joins. Render at 20 or 16 px.

Label the unfamiliar. Icon-only buttons need an aria-label and a tooltip; the SVG is aria-hidden.

Colour follows text. Icons use currentColor, so they take ink, muted or an *-ink in both themes. Never a fill colour.
