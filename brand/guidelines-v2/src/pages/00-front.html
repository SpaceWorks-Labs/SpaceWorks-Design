What changed since v1.

Brand guidelines v2 follow the product as it ships in SpaceWorks 0.12.0. Where v1 and v2 differ, v2 wins.

Areav1 saidv2 saysDecision
TypographyGilroy Regular to Bold for everything; Unbounded for accents.Only the free Gilroy weights ship: ExtraBold 800 for display, Light 300 for light display text. Outfit (OFL) for body, labels and controls. Unbounded stays for one phrase.299
Dark themeDeep Ink and teal as dark surfaces.One solid black neutral family: page #000000, layers 11, 20, 28. Teal, Citron and Orange are accents only.225, 316
LogoStatic masters only.Same masters. The mark now folds shut and opens on hover and keyboard focus (600 + 1600 ms). Tenant logos never animate.362
LoadingNot covered.One loader everywhere: the box that opens. Cold-start splash, page loader, inline spinner, mono in buttons.313, 322
MotionNot covered.Three durations, three curves, distance tokens, a which-animation-where table and a reduced-motion floor.323
Colour rolesSeven colours and four tested pairs.Product tokens for both themes: fills vs inks, line vs outline, status softs, scrim, chart series and loader colours.316, 363
ChartsNot covered.Five bright brand colours as series, the same hex in both themes; a 1 px edge on fills and a 4 px casing under lines carry the contrast.SW-380
StatusAdd a word or icon.The word is mandatory. Small marks are solid in both themes; large callouts go soft in dark.D6
New sections17 pages.Logo in motion, theming, charts, mascot Patch, components, UX patterns, product rules, accessibility, ID cards, signage and social.

Unchanged: the purpose and personality, the seven brand colours, the logo masters, clear space (H ÷ 4), minimum sizes and the voice.
