Why

Gilroy is a commercial family. Its author releases Light and ExtraBold free, under an EULA that allows websites (@font-face), graphics, print and embedding in software. Regular to Bold are paid. The project does not hold that licence, so those files were removed from every repository (decision 299, 11 November 2026) and are never re-added.

What fills the gap

Outfit (SIL OFL, variable 400–700) takes every role between the two Gilroy weights: body, labels, buttons, table text, numbers and IDs. Unbounded (OFL) keeps its one accent phrase.

Never
  • Ask the browser for a Gilroy weight that does not ship. Set font-synthesis: none.
  • Fake a middle weight with a stroke, a shadow or faux bold.
  • Download a paid weight “just for a mock-up”.
